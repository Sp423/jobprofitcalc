Plumbing Pricing Guide

Diagnostics, Trip Fees, and Licensed Rate

Licensed plumbing work supports premium pricing because of diagnostic skill, urgency, and permitting. For example, a $400 plumbing repair does not get shopped the way a $12,000 remodel does. That advantage disappears if the quote is still hours times a familiar rate with no trip fee, no material run, and no overhead.

This page is plumber job pricing in one place: how to price plumbing jobs that include diagnostics, a minimum trip fee, a return for parts, and permit time, then run the same numbers through the calculator above.

What hourly rate should a plumber charge?

Many licensed plumbers advertise a charge somewhere in a wide hourly band, often with a separate trip fee. That advertised number is a billing rate. It is not what belongs in Hourly Labor Rate.

Enter your loaded cost per hour: wage plus payroll taxes, workers' comp, and benefits. Overhead and margin sit on top of that cost. Do not paste a billing rate into the field. Drive labor is drive time times crew size. Owner self-employment tax is estimated on profit and is not added to the price.

Always charge for diagnostic time

Always charge for diagnostic time. Your expertise in finding the problem is worth money, not just fixing it. Diagnostic time is often where you earn your real hourly rate. For example, a leak that takes twenty minutes to repair and forty minutes to find is an hour of licensed work, not a twenty-minute favor.

Minimum trip fee on short calls

Set and enforce a minimum trip fee for service calls under one hour. Quick calls that eat drive time are where small operators bleed margin. Customers already accept travel charges from plumbers, HVAC companies, and electricians every day. The contractors who skip the charge often haven't tested whether customers actually object.

Return trips for materials are billable

If a job requires a return trip for materials, that return is billable time. Include a material-run allowance on any job with custom or specialty parts. The homepage plumber tips put this next to diagnostics and trip fees because it is the same leak: unpaid time that never shows up in hours-times-rate.

Permits and inspections

Permit pulls, code compliance research, and inspection coordination take real time. Build an allowance into any permitted job before you quote it. Pass permit fees through (or add a modest handling charge). Never absorb them — they are a job cost that belongs on the customer's invoice.

Enter the job in the calculator above: hours, crew size, your loaded labor cost (not a billing rate), materials at cost, overhead percent, drive time, fuel, and margin. Price is total cost divided by one minus the margin. Your margin covers profit on materials too. Full walkthrough: how to price a contractor job.

What margin should plumbing work hit?

Published net margins for plumbing are thin. IRS data put plumbing and HVAC corporations at 4.8% net income on receipts, after officer pay (IRS SOI Corporation Complete Report, Table 5.1, tax year 2022, US). Canadian government data put small and mid-size plumbing and HVAC firms at 11.2% in 2024, with owner pay counted as profit at unincorporated firms (ISED Canadian Industry Statistics, Canada). No US survey of plumbing-only margins was found. More on what those numbers measure: contractor profit margins by trade.

Set your own target from your overhead and goals, and enter it in Desired Profit Margin. For example, a shop that pays the owner a wage as part of labor cost and wants 12% left over enters 12.

Check the job, not the average: Enter this service call or this water heater in the plumber job pricing calculator and read the Suggested Charge. It covers labor, drive, materials at cost, overhead, fuel, and your margin. It does not include sales tax or your income tax. If you charge sales tax on materials, turn it on in Quote Settings and the customer total shows it as a separate line.

Plumber job pricing FAQ

How do I price a plumbing job with this calculator?

Plumber is preselected so labor, materials, overhead, and drive time start from typical plumbing defaults. Replace every field with the actual service call or water heater job — hours, parts, trip time — and the tool returns a suggested charge, net profit, and margin.

What hourly rate should a plumber charge?

Charge rates and trip fees vary a lot by market, and this page doesn't quote one. Our guide on how to calculate your contractor hourly rate lists published ranges with their sources and dates. Calculate your own rate from your income target and costs, not from what you've heard others charge. In the calculator, Hourly Labor Rate is your loaded cost per hour, not the rate you charge.

Should plumbers charge a trip fee on short service calls?

Set your minimum trip fee to cover the drive there and back plus the first stretch of on-site time, so a five-minute fix doesn't lose money. Plug your drive time into the calculator to see what that costs you.
