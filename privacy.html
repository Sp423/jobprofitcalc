Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information: When you visit the Site, we and our third-party service providers may automatically collect certain information, including:

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site and understand how it is used. Specifically:

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns and improve the Site. Google Analytics may set cookies on your browser. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

  • Session Cookies: We may use session-level cookies to maintain basic Site functionality. These expire when you close your browser.

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect your experience on this and other websites.

How We Use Collected Information

We use the automatically collected information to:
