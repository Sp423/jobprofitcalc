Quick Tips

  • Drive time is billed once — not multiplied by crew size
  • Mark up materials 15–30%; you manage the supply chain
  • SE tax is 15.3% — price it in or you're giving it away
  • Margins below 10% leave no room for error
  • Overhead grows as your business grows — track it monthly

How It Works

What This Job Profit Calculator Shows You

A job profit calculator is not hours times rate. This tool is the same contractor profit calculator on JobProfitCalc.com — it folds labor, marked-up materials, overhead, billed drive time, fuel, self-employment tax, and state income tax into one suggested charge, then shows the profit and margin that charge actually produces.

Pick a trade to load realistic defaults, then replace every field with the job in front of you. Results update as you type: suggested charge to the customer, net profit, profit margin, and effective dollars per hour after costs. Open the cost breakdown when you want the line items behind that price.

Need a walkthrough of each field, quote settings, and the customer PDF? Read how to use the job profit calculator. For the pricing method behind the math, see how to price a contractor job.

Why It Matters

Price the Job So the Profit Survives

Most underpriced jobs look fine until overhead, unpaid drive time, or SE tax show up. A contractor profit calculator puts those costs on the same screen as the bid so you are not discovering the shortfall after the customer already signed.

Use Copy Internal Quote or Internal PDF for your file. Use Customer Quote or Customer PDF when you want a clean total — loaded labor, materials with markup, travel, and due. Those customer lines disclose how the number was built without printing your full internal cost sheet.
